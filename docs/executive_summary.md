# Executive Summary: Product Line Profitability, Nassau Candy Distributor

**Period:** Jan 2024 – Dec 2025 · **Data:** 9,881 validated order lines · 15 products · 3 divisions

## Headline
Nassau Candy earned **$91,076 gross profit on $138,284 sales (65.9% margin)**, with sales up 44% year on year. Profit depends heavily on one division.

## What we found
- **Chocolate carries the business:** five Wonka Bars deliver 92.7% of sales and 94.9% of profit at a 67.4% margin.
- **High sales are not the problem:** the best-selling products are also highly profitable. Margin trouble sits in small, low-volume lines.
- **Kazookles is the main margin risk:** 7.7% margin; $1,206 in sales produced only $93 in profit.
- **"Other" division lags:** 44.8% margin vs 67.4% for Chocolate.
- **Sugar is negligible:** seven products make up 0.3% of sales.
- **Concentration risk:** California, New York and Texas supply 40% of profit; two factories produce nearly all chocolate.

## What we recommend
1. Reprice or renegotiate the cost of Kazookles; review for discontinuation if neither works.
2. Pilot a 1% price increase on chocolate bars (≈ +$1.3k gross profit, if volume holds).
3. Reprice Lickable Wallpaper and Wonka Gum.
4. Review SweeTARTS, Nerds and Fun Dip for discontinuation.
5. Secure supply at the two chocolate factories and broaden the customer base geographically.
