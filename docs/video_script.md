# Video walkthrough script (about 5 minutes)

**0:00 Intro (20s).** "Hi, I'm [name]. This is my Unified Mentor project: Product Line Profitability and Margin Performance Analysis for Nassau Candy Distributor. The goal is to find which products really make money, not only which ones sell the most."

**0:20 Problem (30s).** "Sales volume can mislead. We want to know which product lines have the best gross margin, whether high-sales products are profitable, how divisions differ, and which products are a margin risk."

**0:50 Data and cleaning (40s).** "The dataset has 10,194 rows. I validated sales, cost and profit, checked that Gross Profit equals Sales minus Cost, and removed 313 duplicate order lines, leaving 9,881 rows. I standardised labels and mapped each product to its factory."

**1:30 Dashboard demo (2 min).** Open the app and show each tab:
- KPI row: sales, profit, 65.9% margin, profit per unit.
- Product Overview: leaderboard and profit contribution; point out Scrumdiddlyumptious at 20.6% of profit and Kazookles at 7.7% margin.
- Division Performance: Chocolate 94.9% of profit; Other division has a profit-share gap.
- Cost vs Margin: scatter; Kazookles is the outlier; show risk flags and move the margin slider.
- Profit Concentration: Pareto, 5 of 15 products give 80%+; dependency alert; state share.
- Use date range, division filter and product search once.

**3:30 Key insights (50s).** "Chocolate delivers 92.7% of revenue and 94.9% of profit. High-selling bars are also profitable. The risk is in small lines: Kazookles at 7.7%, and SweeTARTS, Nerds and Fun Dip below 47%. Product margins are fixed, so changes in profit come from mix and volume."

**4:20 Recommendations (30s).** "Fix Kazookles, test a 1% chocolate price rise, reprice Wallpaper and Gum, review the weak Sugar items, and protect the two chocolate factories."

**4:50 Close (10s).** "Code, paper and dashboard are on GitHub. Thanks for watching."

**Tips:** record at 1080p, keep the dashboard in wide mode, and have the paper link ready in the description.
